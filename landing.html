Don't take our word for it

Nuvola makes a strong claim — that your writing never leaves your device. Here are two ways to check that in about a minute, no technical background required.

Watch the Network tab

  1. Open the app and press F12 (or right-click → Inspect).
  2. Click the "Network" tab in the panel that opens.
  3. Write a diary entry, save it, and ask the coach for feedback.
  4. Look through the requests: you'll see the app's own files and, only if you turned the coach on, requests fetching model files — never one carrying your words.

Turn on airplane mode

  1. Load the app once (and download the coach, if you want it) while connected.
  2. Switch your phone or laptop to airplane mode.
  3. Keep writing, checking in, and asking the coach for feedback.
  4. Everything keeps working — there's nowhere for a request to go.

Questions

Is my writing really private?+

Yes. Entries are stored in your browser's local database. There's no login, no cloud sync, and no server that ever sees your text — see "Prove it yourself" above.

What AI model powers the coach?+

A small open model (Qwen2.5), running in your browser via WebGPU. You can pick a 0.5B version (~450 MB, faster, works on most phones) or a 1.5B version (~1.3 GB, more thoughtful replies, wants a newer device).

Does it work without internet?+

Yes, once loaded. Writing and mood check-ins work with zero downloads. The coach needs a one-time download; after that it works fully offline.

Will it work on an older phone?+

The journal and mood check-ins work everywhere. The AI coach needs a browser with WebGPU (recent Chrome, Edge, or Safari). If your device doesn't support it, the coach quietly stays off — nothing else is affected.

I opened this inside Instagram or Facebook — why is the coach missing?+

Those apps' built-in browsers block the technology the coach needs. Tap the ⋯ menu and choose "Open in Chrome" or "Open in Safari" to unlock it. Journaling still works fine either way.

What does it cost?+

Nothing. There's no account and no subscription — it's a page that runs entirely in your browser.

Can I get my entries out if I switch devices?+

Yes — export a backup file from the "You" tab and import it on another device. Because nothing syncs automatically, this is the one manual step privacy asks of you.
